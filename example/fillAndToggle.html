Fill and Legend Toggle

Fill the area under a trendline, and keep one when its dataset is hidden

Try It

Click a series in the legend:

  • New users (blue, tinted area): the trendline is filled with fillColor. Hide the series and its trendline goes with it
  • Returning users (purple, solid line): the dataset sets alwaysShowTrendline: true, so its trendline stays on the chart even when the series is hidden

Configuration Used

  • fillColor is a trendline option: trendlineLinear.fillColor fills between the line and the bottom of the chart area
  • alwaysShowTrendline is a dataset option, not a trendline option — it sits next to label and data
  • legend entries for trendlines are separate; these two datasets do not add any, so the legend stays short
